# Instructions for AI coding agents

Before doing anything in this repository, read `docs/DEVELOPER-GUIDE.md`
and follow it: it describes the workflow, what you may and may not do
without approval, and the layout's ground rules it points to. Then read
`docs/HANDOFF.md` if it exists, and the backlog in `docs/TODO-features.md`
and `docs/TODO-bugs.md`.
